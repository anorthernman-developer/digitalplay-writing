digitalplay Writing

Words as loose parts: cut them up, constrain them, turn them into games. Part of digitalplay.work.

  • Cut-Up Machine
    Paste in your own words, cut them into pieces with digital scissors, and shuffle until a line you would never have written turns up. Lock the bits you like and shake the rest.
  • Tiny Adventure
    Write a little text adventure and play it straight away. Make rooms, join them with exits, hide objects, set a goal, then type go north and see if your world holds together.
